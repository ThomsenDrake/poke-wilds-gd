### Spawn model — roaming entities on a slot grid, stationary guardians, one biome truth

- **Two entity classes (faithful, `wiki-overworld-encounters.md:222-224`).** (1) ROAMING — "The vast majority of overworld Pokemon wander around freely and can end up in entirely different Biomes" (:224); roamers cross biome boundaries freely (no biome lock — a roamer's cell biome is read where it stands, never pinned at spawn). (2) STATIONARY — "reserved for special encounters such as Legendary Pokemon, revived Fossils, or other unique instances," and unlike roamers "are often capable of being rematched after being KO'd" (:224). Phase 6 ships the stationary class as ONE occupant: the nest guardian Alpha (§ Nests); legendaries stay Phase 7 (the dormant `PKMNMANSION`/`RUINS_*` tokens, `biome_encounters.gd:34-56`). "Eggs are not considered stationary encounters" (:224) — wild eggs are their own entity kind, below.
- **Spawn tables ride `biome_encounters` — one source of biome truth.** Per-biome OVERWORLD tables exist in the original and are DISTINCT from the grass tables (`fresh-beach.md:50` lists "Overworld encounters:" as a flat species list separate from the tall-grass grid :59/:77), but the scrapes give no per-biome overworld MEMBERSHIP beyond examples — so the port reuses `biome_encounters.filter_species_ids(catalog.species, biome, time_of_day)` (`biome_encounters.gd:82-115`, already time-of-day-aware: NIGHT pools gain GHOST, UMBREON is night-only) as the species pool for a cell's biome, the SAME filter `_pick_encounter_species` rides (two encounter sources, one table — no second biome list, the habitat-drops one-scan mandate). `NEVER_ENCOUNTER_IDS` (EGG) and the battle-viability gate apply unchanged. TIME-OF-DAY: the pool is filtered by `DayPhase.time_of_day_label(session.time_of_day_minutes)` at simulation activation and re-filtered on each crossed DAY↔NIGHT boundary (`day_phase.gd`), so night cells admit ghost species the day pool bars — the only documented time condition is the ghost one ("can only trigger when the player is on Damp, Mystic, or Cursed Soil for a certain amount of time," :272-274; those soil tiles do not exist in the port — the Phase-2 night-without-light precedent applies, and the ghost AGGRESSIVE disposition below is the port's ghost-time expression).
- **The slot/cell grid (FLAGGED DESIGN DECISION — the scrapes publish NO density/cap/spawn-trigger).** "despawn" appears 0 times across all scrapes; no density, cap, spawn distance, or respawn rule exists to mine. The port invents a pinned, deterministic model (§ Pinned constants): the world is tiled into `CELL_SIZE := 8`-tile square cells; each cell carries exactly `SLOTS_PER_CELL := 1` roaming slot (a second slot is a flagged one-const flip away). Slot presence is `OverworldMons.is_slot_present(world_seed, cell, slot) := _mix(...) % 100 < SPAWN_PRESENT_PCT` with `SPAWN_PRESENT_PCT := 25` — ~6-9 live roamers inside the 61×41 render window (`world_view.gd:22-23`), the visible-presence density the baselines pin. A cell's biome is its CENTER tile's biome (`world_generator.get_tile_logic` — pure noise, no rng at tile-logic time); roamers crossing into a new cell's biome simply render/aggro per their species-biome disposition (§ Dispositions — disposition resolves on (species, current-biome, time), matching the wiki's biome-conditioned aggressives). Water traversal: `swim_only` mons (14 species in the data) spawn on WATER cells and move only on water tiles; other mons path walkable tiles only (the cross-biome wandering :224 + "Sharpedo in the ocean" :270 imply water traversal for water mons; obstacle rules are otherwise undocumented — flagged).
- **Level scaling (faithful anchor, port band).** "The levels of wild monsters increase the further you explore" (`github-readme.md:201`): an entity's level is a pure function of its tile's Manhattan distance from origin — an ORTHOGONAL progression mechanic to biome assignment (infinite-world slice 2 retired the radial biome bands; the distance gradient stays — `level := clampi(2 + ring/6 + _mix(seed, cell, slot, SALT_LEVEL) % 3, 2, 100)`, `OverworldMons.level_for`). "If the pokemon in your team are high enough level, you might encounter the evolution of the encounters listed" (`fresh-beach.md:48`) is DEFERRED (evolved-variant surfacing needs a pre-evolution query the pool pass doesn't carry — tech debt; flagged).
- **Spawn/despawn lifecycle (FLAGGED — no documented thresholds).** An entity EXISTS when its slot hash gates present AND it has not been removed this session; `overworld_mon_spawned {slot, species_id, tile, disposition, level, is_shiny}` fires when a slot first materializes inside the ACTIVE window (the runtime simulates only the player's window + one-cell margin, so spawn traces are window-entry events, never a world-wide storm). Removal fires `overworld_mon_despawned {slot, species_id, tile, reason}` with `reason` ∈ `"ko"` / `"caught"` (faithful: "KO'ing or catching a Pokemon will remove it from the overworld," :288), `"fled"` (faithful: the timid "run before disappearing from the overworld if you get too close," :254-258), `"distance"` (the invented despawn: a roamer more than `DESPAWN_CELLS := 3` cells from the player's cell unmaterializes — it re-derives identically if the player returns at the same `total_steps` band, since presence is pure), and `"recompute"` (a DAY↔NIGHT pool re-filter dropping a now-illegal species). A player white-out LEAVES every entity in place (faithful :288 — removal rides KO/catch only). Removals are session-scoped (§ Persistence: transient).
- **Roaming cadence (FLAGGED — "wander around freely" is the only statement, :222).** Roamers advance on the PLAYER STEP clock, never wall-clock or `_process` delta: every `ROAM_EVERY := 4` player steps, each live roamer moves one tile to a neighbor drawn from `_mix(world_seed, slot, total_steps / ROAM_EVERY, SALT_MOVE)` over the walkable, UNOCCUPIED 4-neighbors (never the player's tile or another entity's tile — mons never phase through props or stack on an entity; an unmoving roll when every neighbor blocks); the render node lerps between logic tiles for presentation only (logic position is a pure function of the step index, so the audit's model/render cross-check is exact). The one documented per-species speed — "Diglett and Dugtrio move faster than any other Pokemon" (:296) — rides `FAST_ROAM_IDS := {"DIGLETT": true, "DUGTRIO": true}` at `ROAM_EVERY / 2`.

### Dispositions — four canonical behaviors, a radius-triggered state machine

- **The four (faithful, the article's Behavior sections, TOC :200-205).** (1) TIMID (:252-258): "easily spooked, and will run before disappearing from the overworld if you get too close"; preventable via Charm. (2) FRIENDLY (:260-262): "will freely join your party when interacting with them." (3) IRRITABLE (:264-266): "become provoked if you interact with them twice in a row, chasing the player down and initiating battle when you're caught." (4) AGGRESSIVE (:268-270): "will always chase down the player upon being spotted."
- **Species → disposition resolution (PARTIAL data; flagged assumptions).** The catalog's parsed `overworld_behavior` block (`swim_only`/`flee`/`lunge`/`aggression` — carried on the generated catalog entry since the PokeAPI catalog migration, the ASM parser retired) is faithful but near-degenerate (98.7% aggression=1), so the port layers wiki example overrides on top and pins a DEFAULT — flagged per rule: (a) TIMID = the `flee=1` set (EKANS/PIDGEY/RATTATA/SPEAROW/PORYGON/SMEARGLE) + the wiki's named CHANSEY "(though not Blissey)" (:252); (b) FRIENDLY = first-form species (a base species with a non-empty evolution list, catalog-driven — "usually first form Pokemon") + the wiki exceptions JUMPLUFF/BELLOSSOM/BLISSEY/POOCHYENA (:262; the data's `aggression=2` on POOCHYENA is overridden by the explicit wiki membership — flagged); (c) AGGRESSIVE is SPECIES+BIOME+TIME gated exactly as the wiki examples (:268-274): PRIMEAPE on SAVANNA, DRAPION on DESERT, SHARPEDO on WATER (its `lunge=1`/`swim_only` data agrees), GHOST-type species on SWAMP (the GRAVEYARD alias) and FOREST (DEEP_FOREST) at NIGHT only (the ghost time-condition :272-274); (d) IRRITABLE = the DEFAULT for everything else — the wiki's "mostly evolved Pokemon" reading (interact-twice provocation is the safe middle disposition; the data's `aggression=2` PINSIR/TAUROS land here, matching :266's named examples). DUSCLOPS-aggressive ("in the Ruins Underground," :270) is DORMANT until the Phase-7 Ruins landmark (no ruins biome exists; flagged). The FULL per-biome table is not in the scrapes — the resolution function is the single editable home (`OverworldMons.disposition_for(species_id, biome, time_of_day)`), and the scenario asserts the named wiki examples resolve correctly off the live catalog.
- **Radii + the state machine (FLAGGED — no numeric radius anywhere; "upon being spotted" :270 and "if you get too close" :254 are the only words).** States: `IDLE → FLEEING → DESPAWNED` (timid), `IDLE → CHASING → BATTLE` (aggressive/irritable), `IDLE → PACIFIED → IDLE` (Charm). Constants: `SPOT_RADIUS := 6` (Manhattan; aggressive sight range), `SPOOK_RADIUS := 3` (timid flee trigger), `CHASE_RADIUS := 8` (a chase drops beyond this), `CATCH_RADIUS := 1` (adjacency starts the forced battle). The closing chase step that first enters adjacency does not arm the battle; the next player-step clock does, so `entity_layer`'s presentation lerp can settle on the adjacent tile before the encounter starts (otherwise the player sees the mon still two tiles away). TIMID: player within `SPOOK_RADIUS` and not PACIFIED → `FLEEING` (one tile away from the player per player step, `FLEE_STEPS := 6` steps, then `overworld_mon_despawned{reason: "fled"}`). AGGRESSIVE: player within `SPOT_RADIUS` → `CHASING`; path one tile toward the player per player step (fast-gait counter: while the player is mounted (Ride), the chaser moves every OTHER step — "Ride (outrun)" :278 is real by construction; the sim honors the Ride flag only, which the wiki names); adjacency → forced battle (§ Attack); beyond `CHASE_RADIUS` → `IDLE` ("running away will not get the Pokemon to stop chasing you and you have little time before it will start chasing you again," :278 — the re-spot is immediate inside `SPOT_RADIUS`, the cooldown is DEFERRED/flagged). IRRITABLE: the FIRST overworld-Z interact is a warning ("It's eyeing you warily."); the SECOND consecutive interact on the same entity → `CHASING` like an aggressive (the interact counter resets on any other entity's interact or 16 steps without one). Counter-play is the wiki's list (:278): Dig obstructions (a cleared/harvested tile between chaser and player breaks line — the chaser repaths around, buying steps), Ride (the fast-gait half-speed), Fly/Teleport (warp ends every chase — `field_move_actions._warp` clears chase state), Charm (§ Recruitment).
- **The provoked-mon buff (faithful — and the ONLY documented "buffed mon").** A mon that catches the player via chase (aggressive spot OR provoked irritable) enters its battle with physical attack raised "by a staggering 3 stages" (:284); stat changes/status/HP persist across the player running or white-outing (:284), and a white-out "in some cases" leaves an aggressive mon non-hostile afterward (:288 — port: a white-out/escape drops the entity's `CHASING` state to `IDLE` EACH TIME, flagged as the "some cases" reading). PLAYER-INITIATED battle (Attack on a visible mon) "den[ies] the attack stat boost" and lets the player run without being chased after, "even if you damaged it" (:280) — the forced-battle seam carries a `provoked: bool` flag that decides the +3 (`attack_stages` on the enemy instance; `battle_runtime.start_wild_battle` consumes it, 315/320 has the lines).

### Recruitment — dialogue for the Friendly subset, Charm as the level-gated pacifier

- **Path A — dialogue/interact-recruit (faithful).** "Recruit friendly OW (Overworld) Pokemon by interacting with them (Z/…A button). If the Pokemon is friendly, you can have it join your party" (`wiki-getting-started.md:65`); hard rule: "you must have a free space in your party for them to join you, they will not just warp back to your last rest spot" (:262). Overworld `Z` facing a FRIENDLY-disposition entity routes to `overworld_mons_runtime.interact(tile)`: it traces `recruit_attempted {species_id, tile, path: "dialogue", party_free, ok, reason}` and, on a free slot (`session.add_pokemon_to_party`, cap 6), builds the party instance from the entity's SPAWN-TIME rolls (level/gender/shiny — § Determinism; the instance is stamped, never re-rolled), adds it, removes the entity, and traces `recruit_succeeded {species_id, tile, level, is_shiny, party_size}` ("Wild PIDGEY joined your party!"). A full party refuses `recruit_attempted{ok: false, reason: "party_full"}` ("Your party is full. It won't just wait at your camp." — the :262 rule, never silent). NON-friendly interact refuses `reason: "not_friendly"` ("It isn't interested in joining you."); the documented workaround — "having a Dark Pokemon use Attack to force a battle and capture it that way, which WILL warp them back to your last resting spot" (:262) — is exactly the Attack path below (capture overflow rides the existing campsite hold).
- **Path B — Charm (level-gated pacification, then interact).** "You can prevent this by using the field move Charm" (:254); "Charm can prevent the attack altogether if the Pokemon using it is at a high enough level" (:278 — explicitly even an aggressive mon's attack). The Phase-4 seam ALREADY computes the math: `field_move_runtime.use_charm(target, target_level)` sets `pacified := _first_capable_level("charm") >= target_level` (:254 — "more reliably at higher levels" verbatim). Phase 6 plugs `overworld_charm_hook` into the entity layer: a PACIFIED timid never flees (`PACIFIED` holds `CHARM_CALM_STEPS := 12` player steps); a PACIFIED aggressive/irritable drops `CHASING` → `IDLE` for the same window (the :278 "prevent the attack" reading). The recruitable step is THEN the normal dialogue interact — the scrapes frame Charm as flee/attack PREVENTION, not a direct party-add, so the Charm→recruit pipeline (calm, then Z) is the faithful SYNTHESIS; the exact Charm→party step and the numeric threshold beyond the shipped `user_level >= target_level` gate are flagged interpretations. `field_move_actions.gd` (92/220, room) resolves the real target — `overworld_mons_runtime.entity_at(_player.facing_tile())` — replacing today's empty-target placeholder (`_invoke` :59-60, always `no_target`); no target keeps the existing graceful refusal.
- **The recruitable subset == the Friendly disposition** (the wiki's list :262 + the Forest/Savanna Cut-helpful wanderers "Jumpluff… Bellossom" `wiki-getting-started.md:75`). First-form coverage is catalog-driven, so every biome's roamers include recruitable mons by construction — the exit criterion "every biome shows roaming Pokémon with correct dispositions" is witnessed per biome by the scenario's catalog read.

(b) **ATTACK on an egg** (the Attack field move facing a wild egg): "will allow you to check if it's shiny, as well as clear it from the overworld WITHOUT aggravating nearby wild Pokemon" (:248) — `attack_entity` on an egg traces `egg_cleared {tile, species_id, is_shiny}`, shows the shiny verdict ("The egg gleams oddly… It's SHINY!" / "The egg breaks apart. It was not shiny."), removes the egg, and provokes NOTHING. This binary is the Attack-on-egg scenario's witness.

### Rate balance & Repel — both encounter sources coexist (explicit, flagged)

- **Coexistence is faithful; the rates are a port decision (the scrapes quantify nothing).** The original has BOTH roaming visible mons AND biome grass encounters as distinct sources — proven structurally by `fresh-beach.md`'s separate "Overworld encounters" (:50) and "Wild encounters (tall grass)" (:59/:77) tables, corroborated by reviews ("Pokemon both hide in the tall grass, and are randomly wandering around as sprites," `lchan-review.md:11`). The port's EXPLICIT balance (pinned, flagged — no faithful rate exists to match; UPDATED by the configurable-encounters slice, § Configurable encounters): the DEFAULT is now COLLISION-ONLY — a wild battle starts when the player's sprite shares a tile with a wild mon (or an aggressive mon chase-catches); the GRASS random per-step roll is OPT-IN through `session.encounter_settings` (mode `off`/`classic`/`anywhere`; rate ladder 0.02–0.50, step 0.02, 0.12 when on = `SessionState.ENCOUNTER_RATE_DEFAULT`; with NO runtime the gate resolves `{}` == off, i.e. no roll — the legacy `encounter_chance` @export is a dead leftover, not a fallback; the run modifier :25 is unchanged); ROAMING is the persistent visible presence (~6-9 live entities per window via `SPAWN_PRESENT_PCT`). ### Configurable encounters — collision-only default, opt-in random roll (explicit)

- **Default = collision only (the user decision).** With no configuration, wild battles start ONLY when the player's overworld sprite COLLIDES with a wild Pokémon — modeled as a SHARED TILE (the game is physics-free, so "collision" is a tile-coordinate check, not an Area2D). Implemented as the pure domain predicate `scripts/domain/contact_encounter.gd::is_battle_contact(entity)` (`not empty and kind != "egg"`) consulted by `overworld_mons_runtime._check_player_contact(player_tile)` inside `note_player_step` — AFTER `step_triggers` (an already-armed chase-catch keeps first refusal this step), BEFORE roam/sync — and only when the subsystem is `active` (so every non-overworld baseline stays byte-stable by construction). A contact battle is player-initiated: `provoked: false`, NO +3 buff (the `attack_entity` :280 precedent, through the same forced-battle pending seam), the collided mon's species; EGGS never battle on contact (they keep the TAKE/Attack binary :248); the aggressive chase-catch (`provoked: true`, +3) is UNCHANGED. Because chasers/roamers never stand on the player tile, only mons the player steps onto (plus stationary guardians/legendaries) trigger — a mon materializing under a standing player is not a step-onto and never fires, and escaping drops the mon to idle so the next step leaves the tile (no same-tile re-battle loop).
- **The random roll is OPT-IN + configurable (how / where / frequency).** `session.encounter_settings` (v5-ADDITIVE, NO `SAVE_VERSION` bump; `{}`/absent == `"off"`, the canonical byte-preserving default — a never-touched save carries no key) holds `mode` + `rate`: `off` (never roll), `classic` (per-step roll on encounter/tall-grass tiles), `anywhere` (per-step roll on any standable tile); `rate` snaps to the 0.02–0.50 ladder (step 0.02). `player_avatar._try_trigger_encounter` reads the settings live from `/root/GameRuntime` (the `_try_edge_cross` autoload precedent) and gates the roll — `off` returns before any rng is touched, so the avatar's trigger stream (and the pinned scenarios that ride it) is untouched; changes take effect on the next step. The mode/rate constants live on `SessionState` (single source); the roll still rides the avatar's own `_rng` and `generate_wild_encounter` (so Repel wards it exactly when opted in), and the shared `game_runtime._rng` wild stream stays provably unconsumed (contact rides the pending seam, not the draw).
- **Configuration surface = the title-screen Options menu** (`menu-and-save.md` § Options): a StartMenu child submenu (`scripts/ui/options_screen.gd` + `scenes/ui/OptionsScreen.tscn`) cycles OFF/CLASSIC/ANYWHERE and nudges the rate, write-through + save on every change (the session is the single source). The setting persists across new games within a session as a player preference (it is NOT reset by `reset_for_new_game`); a fresh boot with no prior setting defaults to `off`.

## Determinism (load-bearing — the night_system guarantee holds)

EVERY entity roll is a pure function of `(world_seed, total_steps, slot)` on a DERIVED hash stream — the shared `_rng` (the wild-encounter stream; `night_system.gd:24-28`, the habitat/harvest headers) is NEVER consumed by this subsystem, so every other pinned headless scenario (the 39 non-opt-in), the determinism pins/canary, and the `shiny_odds` 6/1024 expectation are untouched by construction.

- **The hash.** `OverworldMons._mix(world_seed, a, b, salt)` copies the `harvest_resolver._dig_draw` shape verbatim (`harvest_resolver.gd:99-103`): a SplitMix-style integer mix with explicit sub-`INT64_MAX` constants + 63-bit masks, int64 multiply-wrap (deterministic two's-complement), NO engine `hash()` (no Godot-version drift), NO dict iteration anywhere. Salts separate streams: `SALT_PRESENT` / `SALT_SPECIES` / `SALT_LEVEL` / `SALT_MOVE` / `SALT_SHINY` / `SALT_GENDER` / `SALT_NEST` / `SALT_EGG` — distinct odd constants so no two streams correlate.
- **Per-roll mapping.** presence = `_mix(seed, cell.x, cell.y·slot, SALT_PRESENT) % 100 < SPAWN_PRESENT_PCT`; species index = `_mix(seed, cell.x, cell.y·slot, SALT_SPECIES) % pool.size()` over the SORTED `filter_species_ids` result (sorted ⇒ deterministic membership); level/gender/shiny per § Supported behavior (shiny = `_mix(...) % 256 == 0` — exactly 1/256; gender = `_mix(...) % 256` against the species ratio); roam neighbor = `_mix(seed, slot, total_steps / ROAM_EVERY, SALT_MOVE)` over the walkable, unoccupied neighbors minus the player tile (the candidate list shrinks when a neighbor is blocked/occupied — still a pure function of the fixed input script, so the timeline stays fixed); nest + nest-eggs the same shape on `SALT_NEST`/`SALT_EGG`. Player-TRIGGERED transitions (aggro start step, flee start, interact count) are input-driven, exactly like the player's Attack choice — given a fixed `(world_seed, input script)`, `total_steps`'s trajectory is fixed, so the entire entity timeline is fixed.
- **Movement is step-indexed, never frame-timed.** Roam/flee/chase positions advance on `total_steps` (§ Spawn/Dispositions); the render node's lerp is presentation only. No `_process(delta)` logic, no wall-clock read — a double-process headless run is byte-identical.
- **Proof sketch (pinned in the scenario).** (1) Two same-seed same-step-script runs yield an identical `entity_set_hash` (species+tile+disposition+shiny over sorted slots) at every sampled step. (2) CONTROL: N `generate_wild_encounter` draws on a pinned seed with entities ACTIVE yield the identical species/level/shiny sequence as with the subsystem inert — the wild stream is provably unconsumed. (3) The determinism canary (`determinism_verify.py`) and every existing scenario's rng pin need NO change: the subsystem holds no `RandomNumberGenerator` reference at all (static pure domain + a runtime that reads `session.total_steps`/`world_seed` only).
- **Activation gate (baseline protection).** `overworld_mons_runtime.active` defaults TRUE (normal play has roaming mons from boot). The smoke dispatcher flips it FALSE for every scenario that does not opt in (`smoke_scenario_runner.gd` spends its ONE free line (319/320) on the opt-out; opt-in set = `{overworld_mons, visual_sweep_overworld, visual_sweep_overworld_update}`). Inert ⇒ no entity ticks, no entity traces, no entity sprites ⇒ all 39 non-opt-in headless scenarios keep their exact trace streams and all 24 pre-existing committed baselines (crafted at `total_steps=0`, `visual_sweep_baselines.gd:46-62`) stay BYTE-IDENTICAL; the new 22/23 baselines are strictly additive (26 on disk once committed) (the low-risk reading of the plan's "additive baselines").
