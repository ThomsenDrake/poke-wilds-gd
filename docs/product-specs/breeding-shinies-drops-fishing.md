# Pokémon Systems: Shinies, Breeding, Habitat Drops & Fishing

Phase 5 (feature-completion plan features #13, #14, #15 + the shiny half of #5) lands the breed→hatch→shiny-hunt loop, the FAITHFUL happy-Pokémon habitat drops (the primary material source the Phase-2 interim battle drops stood in for), and rod-tiered fishing — composed over landed dependencies: Phase-1 fences (pens), Phase-2 rods (Old/Good/Super craft at the campfire), Phase-2 happiness + time-evolution context, and Phase-4 Smash (the demolition-witness reconciliation below). Faithful sources are the local wiki scrapes (`.firecrawl/wiki-breeding.md`, `wiki-faq.md`, `wiki-habitat.md`, `wiki-materials.md`, `wiki-overworld-encounters.md`, `wiki-campfire.md`, `wiki-sleeping-bag.md`, `fresh-beach.md`, `github-readme.md`); every divergence from them is named where it is decided.

The subsystem splits into PURE-DOMAIN rules — `scripts/domain/breeding.gd` (pairing, Ditto rule, egg payload + hatch data, the shared pen enclosure detection), `scripts/domain/habitat_drops.gd` (type→tile habitat table, type→material drop table + species overrides, the witness audit, AND the ONE shared habitat scan — `pen_habitat_tags`, interior + one-tile ring — the breeding lay gate rides it too, one table never two), `scripts/domain/fishing.gd` (rod tiers, bite chances, tier pools) — and three runtimes: `scripts/runtime/breeding_runtime.gd` (pen flood-fill detection, pasture deposit/withdraw/egg pickup, the lay + hatch ticks), `scripts/runtime/habitat_runtime.gd` (comfort evaluation, the crossed-day-boundary happiness/drop tick), and `scripts/runtime/fishing_runtime.gd` (rod pick, water check, bite roll, tiered encounter). The evolution-stone bag-use seam rides the NEW `scripts/runtime/stone_evolution_runtime.gd` (validate → consume → swap → trace; stats recompute off the target entry, the level-path parity) — `game_runtime.gd` instantiates + `setup()`s it beside the other runtimes and exposes the one-line `use_stone_on_mon` delegation; the bag screen's stone branch confirms through the autoload (the sleeping-bag convention). All three Phase-5 runtimes were EXTRACTED new because the budget-owned files they touch must not grow: `game_runtime.gd` (320 budget) gains only same-line instantiation + `setup()` beside the other runtimes (the shared `_rng` injected into breeding/fishing — habitat is step-driven and needs no rng — so `seed_for_smoke` pins every roll), the fishing pending-encounter seam in `generate_wild_encounter`, the per-step tick delegation (`note_player_step`), the `use_stone_on_mon` stone seam, and the `fish_caught` capture trace; `main.gd` (220/220) gets same-line wiring only. The shiny roll itself lives in `scripts/domain/pokemon_rules.gd` (the `shiny_odds` static, default 256; `roll_shiny(rng)`, the additive `is_shiny` instance key — extract a helper first if the key does not fit the 320 budget).

## Supported behavior

### Shinies — 1/256 at instance creation, visible in battle/status/egg, faithful overworld silence

- **Odds + roll.** Every Pokémon instance carries an additive `"is_shiny": bool`, rolled at CREATION inside `create_pokemon_instance` (the new optional `rng` parameter): `PokemonRules.roll_shiny(rng)` is `rng.randi_range(1, SHINY_ODDS) == 1` when the shared rng is injected (wild encounters, starters, eggs via the breeding runtime) and falls back to a deterministic hash of the per-instance gender nonce when no rng is passed (legacy scenario/visual fixtures ONLY — every production creation path, fishing included, threads the shared rng; still exactly 1/256, still deterministic). `seed_for_smoke` pins the shared `_rng`, so every injected draw is a pure function of (code, save, seed). The FAQ's "This may be adjustable by the user in a subsiquent update" is honored: the odds live in a single `PokemonRules.shiny_odds` static (default 256) that `Breeding.set_shiny_odds(n)` re-points (validated ≥ 1) — the domain hook the `shiny_odds` scenario proves; a user-facing odds MENU entry is future UI, not a silent gap. `normalize_loaded_mon` backfills an absent `is_shiny` → `false` (additive, NO save bump) and preserves the flag through every load path; evolution's front/back path swap (`battle_runtime._try_evolve`) keeps `is_shiny` (the evolved mon renders its OWN species' shiny palette), and a caught shiny keeps its flag (`use_pokeball` carries wild flags into the party mon). `shiny_rolled {species_id, is_shiny, odds, origin}` fires on EVERY instance creation (`origin` ∈ `"wild"` / `"starter"` from GameRuntime, `"egg"` at lay time from BreedingRuntime, `"fish"` at hook-up from FishingRuntime — a hatch carries its lay-time flag, no separate emission), so the odds are provable in BOTH directions and the flag is observable in scenario play.
- **Battle render = palette swap + one-shot sparkle.** A shiny in battle renders the species' alternate PALETTE ("shiny colors" per the asset credits, `github-readme.md:460`) — NOT a particle effect. `battle_surface.gd`'s sprite selection (`_apply_snapshot` :173-174 → `battle_surface_layout.pokemon_frame` :143-160) keys on `mon["is_shiny"]`: a shiny frame helper parses the species folder's `front.pal` + `shiny.pal` (two `RGB r,g,b` lines each, 0-31 GBC scale), remaps the EXACT two `front.pal` colors to the two `shiny.pal` colors over the full strip Image BEFORE the AtlasTexture first-frame crop (black outline + white highlight + alpha pass through; unmatched pixels pass through untouched), and caches per path in the existing `_frame_cache`. Back sprites ride the same pal files. A one-shot GSC-style sparkle (`pokewilds/shiny_inverse.png`, 8x8 white sparkle on transparent) plays over the sprite at battle start. **Fallback rule (documented):** species WITHOUT a `shiny.pal` (354 of 990 folders) render the normal sprite + the sparkle + the status badge — the `is_shiny` flag is still real for odds/breeding/eggs; only the recolor is absent. Exact-match-only remap means an unverified sprite with extra/anti-aliased colors partial-remaps safely rather than corrupting.
- **Status / party / egg = shiny icon badge.** The shiny status symbol is `pokewilds/shiny.png` / `shiny_inverse.png` at the asset-dump root (committed upstream as "+shiny icon for status screen"; two variants = black-on-white for light panels, white-on-transparent for dark). Party rows (`party_rows.gd`) and the egg slot render the badge beside the gender symbols; the egg's status screen shows it "next to gender in the top right corner" BEFORE hatch (faithful, `wiki-breeding.md:50`) — the egg dict carries `is_shiny` at the top level AND inside its `egg` payload, both rolled at lay time.
- **Overworld render (documented decision, resolves exec plan line 146).** The original renders shinies IDENTICALLY in the overworld: "They will not appear any different if they are shiny until the player engages in battle with them or checks their stats from the party menu" (`wiki-overworld-encounters.md:230`). The port satisfies this STRUCTURALLY for wild Pokémon (random grass encounters — no overworld mon ENTITIES until Phase 6). Where the port DOES render an overworld sprite, a shiny swaps `overworld-shiny.png` when it exists (579 of 990 folders carry a full recolor of the 96x16 six-frame sheet; verified gyarados blue→red) and falls back to the normal sheet otherwise: the Ride mount (`player_avatar.gd`), and the pen EGG entity (`breeding_runtime.ground_egg_at` → the world view renders the gold-egg frames, and a shiny ground egg reports `is_shiny: true` to the view for its badge). Phase-6 overworld mons inherit the same swap rule. An always-on overworld sparkle for WILD mons would be a divergence and is deliberately NOT built.

### Breeding — penned pairs, egg groups, Ditto, father egg moves, pre-hatch egg visibility

- **Pens = sealed Phase-1 fence rings.** A pen is a BOUNDED walkable region detected by ONE SHARED flood both runtimes ride (`Breeding.detect_pens` / `flood_region` / `anchor_key` in `scripts/domain/breeding.gd`, called from `breeding_runtime._detect_regions` AND `habitat_runtime.pen_region_for`, `PEN_FLOOD_BUDGET := 256` — a flood reaching the budget is unbounded and yields no pen), so habitat + breeding never disagree about what counts as a pen. Fences are solid, so a pen is a COMPLETE fence ring: a door-between-fences is walkable, so the flood escapes and a gated ring is NOT a pen (faithful: an open gate is no enclosure; mons do not need gates because the port has no walking penned mons — Phase 6 owns mon entities). The pen anchor (its session key) is the deterministic minimum interior tile — min y, then x (`Breeding.anchor_key`; ONE convention for both runtimes). The would-trap guard keeps the player out of a sealed ring, so all pasture interaction reaches IN from outside.
- **The pasture abstraction (no overworld-mon entities until Phase 6).** Each pen carries a PASTURE: `{"mons": [...], "eggs": [...]}` under ONE additive session key (Persistence, below), held BY REFERENCE: `breeding_runtime._pastures` IS `_session.pastures` (bound at `setup`, self-healed via `is_same()` at the `tick` head and in `_pasture` whenever `session.reset`/New Game assigns a fresh dict). `breeding_runtime` owns the LIVE pasture operations (deposit/withdraw/lay/pickup); `habitat_runtime` reads AND WRITES the SAME dicts in place on its daily comfort/happiness/drop tick — so the +10 happiness a penned mon accrues reaches breeding's 220 lay gate the SAME step (habitat ticks before breeding, `game_runtime.note_player_step`), with NO save roundtrip and no copy. (An earlier revision mirrored through a deep-copied session key and claimed the battle-end autosave re-synced it — that was false; the reference-identity store replaced it.) **Deposit** a party member via `breeding_runtime.deposit_to_nearest_pen` (the party-screen DROP action — standing next to a pen; refuses `no_pen` / `no_such_mon` / `last_party_member` / `eggs_stay_with_you` with player-facing messages, `mon_penned` on success, `breeding_refused` otherwise); `habitat_runtime.deposit_mon` is the parallel drop-economy seam (evaluates comfort AT DEPOSIT — the faithful drop-time check — and stamps the mon's cached `habitat` state, `pasture_deposited`). **Withdraw**: overworld `Z` facing a pen interior tile (`breeding_runtime.interact`) picks up a ground EGG first (priority — `egg_picked_up` into a party slot, `party_full` refused), else withdraws the most-recently penned mon (`mon_withdrawn`, party cap 6 — a refusal leaves the mon penned, never lost), else reports "The pen is quiet."; `habitat_runtime.withdraw_mon` is the anchor-keyed seam (it erases the shared pasture entry only when BOTH mons AND eggs are gone — `breeding_runtime`'s fence-Z withdraw applies the SAME rule — so a withdrawal can never destroy breeding's ground eggs, and an emptied pen leaves no phantom entry behind). A pen tile that is not a pen interior returns `{}` so the router falls through to harvest/build unchanged.
- **Pairing rule.** A female + a compatible male sharing an EGG GROUP breed (`Breeding.can_breed` / `find_pair`; egg groups parsed at `species_file_parser.gd:114-118` — `dn EGG_*`, prefix stripped — and carried on the catalog entry :183). Offspring species = the MOTHER's. **Ditto exception:** Ditto (`EGG_DITTO`, `GENDER_UNKNOWN`) breeds with any breedable species in either direction; when the partner is female the partner lays, else the Ditto lays (Ditto-as-mother → the non-Ditto parent's species is the offspring — the wiki's recommended pen layout). Ditto never breeds with Ditto. EXACTLY ONE compatible pair per pen breeds: `find_pair` returns a pair only when it is the ONLY compatible pair (`pairs.size() == 1`) — a third compatible mon breaks attraction (the faithful "wander too far" separation rule) and the pen produces nothing until the extra mon leaves; extra INCOMPATIBLE mons form no pair and are ignored.
- **UNBREEDABLE — const-toggleable documented divergence.** The faithful original does NOT breed genderless/Undiscovered/legendaries, even with Ditto (`wiki-breeding.md` Note b, v0.8.9-0.8.11: Beldum/Unown cannot breed "with either a Ditto or a Pokemon of their own egg group"). The port ships the FAITHFUL default `Breeding.ALLOW_UNDISCOVERED_BREEDING := false` — such pairs produce nothing, witnessed by `breed_flow`'s unbreedable group; the exec plan's breedable workaround stays one flagged const flip away (never silent).
- **Habitat gate (shared with drops).** Breeding gates on the SAME habitat-tile system as drops (below) — literally one scan, never two tables: `breeding_runtime`'s lay tick calls `HabitatDrops.pen_habitat_tags` (the pen interior PLUS its one-tile 4-neighbor ring, deduped) and evaluates `HabitatDrops.types_satisfied` PER PARENT (OR within a type's tags, AND across a mon's types, the Dragonite-line water override included), AND'ing both parents' verdicts — the exact scan + verdict `habitat_runtime`'s comfort check rides, so "drops-comfortable ⟺ the lay gate passes" holds by construction. (The original revision carried a divergent second table in `breeding.gd` — plural tags, no campfire — which made flying/water/fire pairs unlayable in pens the drops subsystem called comfortable; it is deleted.) Both mons must also be HAPPY (`BREED_HAPPY_THRESHOLD := 220` — the existing `HAPPINESS_EVOLUTION_THRESHOLD`, which also feeds the wired happiness evolution, so a penned happy mon can happiness-evolve on its next level-up). A pen missing its tiles = no eggs AND no drops — one subsystem.
- **Attraction → egg (cadence model).** The port abstracts the original's heart→invisible-countdown→egg-on-moved-tile into a seeded cadence: every `LAY_CHECK_STEPS := 120` steps (≈ two in-game hours) each pen with ≥2 mons, EXACTLY ONE compatible pair (a third breaks attraction), both happy, habitat satisfied, and fewer than `EGG_GROUND_CAP := 7` ground eggs rolls `LAY_CHANCE := 0.5` on the shared rng (the Eevee-farm anchor ≈ 6 eggs / <15 min; a per-species hatch-cycle spread is future data work). The egg spawns on a seeded empty interior tile and renders as a visible pen entity (`ground_egg_at` → world view; the `20_egg_in_pen` baseline). At 7 ground eggs the producer is "self-trapped": laying halts until eggs are picked up (faithful cap).
- **Egg contents are determined AT LAY TIME and VISIBLE before hatch (faithful).** `Breeding.build_egg` returns the party-slot shell `{species_id: "EGG", name: "Egg", is_egg: true, is_shiny, max_hp: 0, current_hp: 0, egg: {species_id, display_name, gender, is_shiny, moves, father_moves, steps_to_hatch}}` — gender (species-ratio roll), moveset, and shiny flag are ALL visible on the egg's status screen before hatch, and `max_hp/current_hp == 0` makes the healthy-party scans skip eggs NATURALLY (shape-guarded). **CRITICAL:** an egg is NEVER built via `create_pokemon_instance(get_species("EGG"))` — the `egg/` folder's base_stats declares ZUBAT garbage; that entry is SPRITE-ONLY (the gold egg frames), and hatching uses the REAL species entry (the catalog already excludes `EGG` from encounters, :201).
- **Egg moves come from the FATHER.** The confirmed wiki rule: the father's KNOWN moves that the child species lists as egg moves pass at hatch, in the father's move order (`Breeding._inherited_egg_moves`). The data existed UNPARSED at `pokewilds/pokemon/egg_moves.asm` (`SpeciesEggMoves:` label + `db MOVE_ID` lines, 359 of 990 folders) — Phase 5 adds a static `parse_egg_moves` to `species_file_parser.gd` (sibling to its other asm reads; absent file → `[]`; malformed lines skipped per the parser's partial-result convention) and an `"egg_moves"` field on the catalog species entry. The moveset fills to four with the child's earliest level-up moves (≤ the hatch level), then `TACKLE` as last resort (`_fill_moveset`); species without parsed egg-move data degrade to that plain low-level moveset (documented).
- **Hatching = carry and WALK.** Party eggs advance one `steps_to_hatch` per player step (the per-step tick wired through `game_runtime.note_player_step` → `breeding_runtime.tick` → `_tick_party_eggs`); ground eggs in a pen do NOT incubate (faithful: "you must walk with it for a while"). At 0 the egg hatches in-place in the party slot into `Breeding.hatch_egg`'s result — a REAL species instance at `HATCH_LEVEL := 5` overridden with the stored gender/is_shiny/moves — with `egg_hatched` (the shiny flag carries from the lay-time `shiny_rolled{origin: "egg"}` — no separate hatch emission). `steps_to_hatch` = the species' `egg_hatch_steps` catalog field when parsed, else `DEFAULT_STEPS_TO_HATCH := 2560` (Gen-2 minimum hatch cycles — a DOCUMENTED ASSUMPTION; hatch-cycle data is not in the parsed asm dump, so the per-species Eevee/Dratini incubation spread awaits that data work).

### Habitat happiness & drops — the FAITHFUL primary material source

- **The interim-vs-faithful relationship (documented).** Phase 2 shipped `material_drops.gd` battle-end type drops (5 materials, 100% on a type-matched victory/capture) as a documented INTERIM so crafting was self-sufficient before this system existed. Phase 5 lands the FAITHFUL primary source — happy penned Pokémon dropping materials (`wiki-materials.md:301`, README:242: "capturing pokemon and dropping them in an area they are happy in, and talking to them") — and the interim FLIPS to a documented SECONDARY source: `material_drops.gd` is KEPT (battle-end drops still grant; its header flips from "Phase 5 replaces this" to "secondary"), never deleted. Both sources ride the SAME witness invariant (below).
- **Habitat tiles (type → required pen tile).** A penned Pokémon is comfortable only if its pen contains tiles matching EACH of its types' requirements (`HabitatDrops.TYPE_HABITATS` + `tile_habitat_tags`, read off `get_tile_logic` over the pen interior PLUS its one-tile 4-neighbor ring — `HabitatDrops.pen_habitat_tags`, deduped; solid props and unwalkable water/lava are never interior tiles, so the ring catches them — the ONE scan shared by this comfort check AND the breeding lay gate). Dual-types need BOTH (FAQ: Chikorita→Tall Grass only, Jumpluff→Tall Grass AND Trees). Non-basic requirements are OR-lists per type (FIRE = lava OR campfire); BASIC ground is RECESSIVE — an empty requirement list means the pen floor suffices, and it is required only when ALL of the mon's types are basic-family (FAQ: Gardevoir Psychic/Fairy → Basic only). From the default 70, a comfortable mon reaches the 220 happy gate in 15 in-game days (the design first sketched an hourly +4/-2 tick; the landed daily cadence is ~9.5x slower — documented pacing the once-per-day drop rhythm rides). The 220 threshold is the happy gate for BOTH breeding and drops.

- **WITNESS INVARIANT reconciliation (load-bearing).** The building spec's region-enclosure escape rides on `build_runtime.unwitnessed_demolish_moves()` staying empty — every shell class has a cost material only its demolish move yields (log→Cut, hard_stone→Smash), so no non-harvest source may yield log or hard_stone (`material_drops.gd:19-24`, asserted every `craft_flow` run). The habitat table joins the guarantee mechanically: `HabitatDrops.witness_clean()` (+ `is_habitat_drop_material`) asserts neither `log` nor `hard_stone` comes from the habitat economy, on every `habitat_drops` run, so a future table slip fails loudly rather than seeding a permanent self-trap. (Phase-4 Smash does not retire the invariant: Smash yielding hard_stone is the WITNESS, not a violation.)

### Fishing — crafted rods, tier-gated water encounters

- **Rods.** Old/Good/Super Rods already craft at the campfire into the bag (Phase 2, `recipes.gd:40-42` — exact wiki match: Old = 1 Log + 1 Silky Thread; Good = 1 Old Rod + 2 Metal Coat; Super = 1 Good Rod + 3 Magnet). They are tools, not structures (`wiki-sleeping-bag.md` "Fishing Rods").
- **Trigger.** Face a water tile and press `Z` with a rod in the bag (the `field_action_router` fishing arm; a bag USE entry on a rod is NOT wired — the overworld Z is the only trigger): `fishing_runtime.try_fish` pre-gates on the faced tile's `WATER` biome / surf gate (water tiles are `walkable=false, encounter=false`, `biome_defs.gd:62`), so fishing works FROM SHORE without a Surf-capable mon (faithful: "used to catch Pokemon by any body of water"). The BEST bagged rod casts automatically (`Fishing.best_rod`: Super > Good > Old). A cast rolls a BITE on the shared seeded rng — `BITE_CHANCE` {Old 0.55, Good 0.70, Super 0.85} (pinned design constants; the wiki describes NO minigame, so the wait is a single roll) — traced `fishing_cast {rod_id, tier, tile, bite}` on every rng-consuming cast; a miss shows "Not a bite... The water settles." (`no_bite`, no encounter). Refusals trace `fishing_refused {reason, rod_id}` — `no_rod` ("You need a Fishing Rod to fish here."), `no_water` (defensive — the router pre-gates), `no_species` (the viability filter emptied the pool) — never silent.
- **Tier-gated encounter table.** A bite draws from the rod tier's CUMULATIVE pool (each tier ADDS, never replaces — better rod → strictly better mons) filtered to battle-viable species (the catalog encounter gate — sprites/catch rate/learnset, the same gate wild draws use):

| Rod (tier) | Pool | Bite | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
